## Memory Freshness owner and F1 evidence

P2’s fixed-threshold independent-event scheduler and counterless accepted-use candidate path are owned by `src/freshness/propagation.rs` and `src/runtime_inference/freshness_access.rs`; public stats paths remain the diagnostic boundary. The [P2 verification report](freshness-performance-p2-verification-2026-10-03.md) records P2 acceptance and its propagation-stage scope. P4 receipt lookup is owned by `src/freshness_storage.rs` and `src/freshness/freshness_storage_membership.rs`; its rebuild/eviction/failure contracts are tested at the storage interface. `scripts/freshness_history_profile.py` owns matched diagnostic collection; [P4 verification](freshness-performance-p4-verification-2026-10-04.md) owns the frozen diagnosis and final acceptance evidence.

The F1 source audit distinguishes **creation at first accepted MemoryStore publication**, exposed by grouped Insomnia completion (`src/owner_lifecycle/insomnia_application.rs`) and direct owner publication (`src/memory/memory_store.rs`), from ordinary **Web admission at first successful settled Dream lifecycle promotion to nonarchived `knowledge`/`canonical`**, including no-link settlement (`src/runtime_inference/dream_lifecycle.rs`). An explicitly active first direct publication may admit immediately.
